# Opportunity Meeting: Kai v2
**Subject:** From Assistant to Agentic Orchestration  
**Date:** March 2, 2026

---

## 1. Objective & Agenda

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Goal:** Gain buy-in for pivoting Kai from a passive "chat assistant" to an active "Agentic Orchestration Platform" integrated directly into developer and marketer workflows.

**Agenda:**
1. Problem Statement: The "Implementation Gap"
2. Why address it now? (Market timing & OKRs)
3. Competitive Benchmark: Optimizely Opal vs. Growthbook MCP
4. Proposed Solution: The 3 Stages of Kai v2
5. Decision

---

## 2. Problem Statement

### User Persona: **The "Last Mile" Implementer**
*   **Developers:** Tired of switching between IDE and Kameleoon to create flags/types.
*   **Product Managers:** Wanting to go from "Idea" to "Live Experiment" without waiting for a sprint.

### Problem Description
> **"As a developer, when I get a suggestion from Kai, I still have to manually create the feature flag, define the types, and write the boilerplate code in my IDE."**

Today, Kai stops at **suggestions**. This creates a "friction-heavy" transition where users must manually bridge the gap between AI advice and code implementation, slowing down the experimentation cycle.

---

## 3. Why Kai v2?

### Opportunity Type: **Competitive Leap**
*   **Justification:** Our competitors are becoming **implementers**. Optimizely is launching agentic flows; Growthbook is owning the IDE with their MCP server.
*   **OKR Connection:** 
    *   **Time to First Experiment:** Target 50% reduction.
    *   **Active User Retention (AUR):** Deepen integration into DAILY workflows (IDE).

### Expected Impact
*   **OKR Contribution:** 25% increase in experiment velocity.
*   **User Benefit Impact:** **5/5** (Removes the most painful manual steps).
*   **Business Impact:** **4/5** (Defensive move against Growthbook; offensive move for differentiation).

## 5. Proposed Solution: 3 Stages

### **Lot 1: The IDE Integration (The "Last Mile")**
*   **Action:** Launch the **Kameleoon MCP Server**.
*   **Features:** Create flags, generate TypeScript types, and check flag status directly from Cursor/VS Code.
*   **Timeline:** Q3 | **Tier:** T1

### **Lot 2: Specialized Agentic Skills**
*   **Action:** Introduce **Kai Skills**.
*   **Features:** "SEO Auditor Agent", "Design-to-Code Agent", and "Experiment Strategy Agent".
*   **Timeline:** Q4 | **Tier:** T1

### **Lot 3: Autonomous Orchestration (Autopilot)**
*   **Action:** **Kai Flow Orchestrator**.
*   **Features:** Kai plans a multi-step experiment, creates flags, writes code, and launches holdouts—waiting ONLY for human approval.
*   **Timeline:** H1 2026 | **Tier:** T3
